[ UIUX design community ]

The design community was founded by Khac Mingg Nguyen - a UI/UX designer living in Vietnam. We maintain a spirit of openness, sharing, and helping each other grow in our careers and even in life. The two main platforms we use are Discord and Patreon.

[ Mingg Patreon ]

Chande.

[ Chande by Mingg Patreon ]

Chande is our community's annual event, where each year brings different formats to infuse freshness and creativity into the design community. We do not follow the beaten path; we strive for freedom and seriousness in design.

[ Chande season 01 ]

Amid the relentless (rise of AI). we long for a space rooted in nature, a place of deep (stillness). Here we slow down together, share & listen to the stories of the past year. Design is not merely a profession. It is a (way of life). Cherished.

We don't say who willbe replaced (by AI).We listen to each individual.

Who is includedin this event
