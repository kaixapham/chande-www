(Vision & Mission)

Through countless events and the dedication of its core members, the Mingg Patreon Discord has become a place that brings people together: to talk design, & to share every other part of life too.

We’re building a community where anyone can share, connect, and learn, whatever their level. All it takes is a positive spirit and a willingness to grow, and our members will always be there to help you get better.
